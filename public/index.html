Smart Investments (30% Wealth Generation Pillar)

Educational guide on building long-term wealth through compounding and disciplined investing.

⚠️ For financial literacy and educational guidance only. Not registered investment advice.

Key Educational Investment Principles

📈

Mutual Funds & SIPs

Systematic monthly investing harnesses the power of compounding and rupee-cost averaging.

📊

Index Funds (Passive)

Low-cost funds that replicate market indices like Nifty 50 for broad market growth.

🛡️

PPF & Debt Instruments

Government-backed instruments offering capital safety, fixed returns, and tax benefits.

🪙

Sovereign Gold Bonds & Gold

Hedge against inflation with sovereign guarantee and 2.5% annual interest.

⚖️

Risk vs. Return & Horizon

Equity for long-term (>5 yrs); Debt/Liquid for short-term (<3 yrs). Match investments with time horizon.

🌐

Asset Diversification

Don't put all eggs in one basket. Balance equity, debt, and gold to manage portfolio risk.

Terms of Use, Privacy Policy & FAQ
1. Terms of Use

Vridhi (वृद्धि) is an educational financial planning and literacy platform designed to help users budget, track milestones, and simulate hypothetical scenarios. By using this service, you acknowledge that all figures, scores, and projections are educational estimates and do not constitute certified financial or investment advisory.

2. Privacy Policy

Your personal information, financial data, and survey scores are securely associated with your authenticated session. Vridhi adheres to strict data privacy principles — we do not sell, monetize, or expose individual financial metrics to third-party commercial entities.

3. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: What is the 20-20-30-30 framework?
A: It is Vridhi's educational allocation model: 20% Secure Savings, 20% Emergency Shield, 30% Home & Essentials, and 30% Wealth Generation.

Q: How do I generate my Monthly Financial Plan?
A: From the Dashboard, click "View Report" or "Download Plan" on the My Financial Report card to view and export your A4 PDF.

Q: Is my emergency fund coverage calculated automatically?
A: Yes! Vridhi evaluates your current savings against 3-month and 6-month essential expense targets in real time.
